# Demo script: "Alex isn't sure where to start" (≈2½ minutes)

**Setup:** run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5173 at about 1440×900. Open **Demo settings** and check the **Conversation engine** box:
- **Live AI (Gemini)** (requires `GEMINI_API_KEY` in `.env.local`): Sunny's wording will vary slightly from the lines below. Rehearse once first, and pace your messages: the free tier has per-minute limits. If you see "free-tier usage limit" or "high demand", wait a minute and press **Try again**.
- **Scripted demo**: the wording matches the lines below. If you record in this mode, say it is scripted; the chat header shows "Scripted demo".

Choose **Alex: three-year home goal**, then close the drawer.

---

### 1. Alex starts unsure (0:00–0:20)
**Screen:** dashboard → **Explore with Sunny**.
**Say:** "Alex is 27, has some savings and wants a first home, but doesn't know where to start. Instead of a form, Sunny asks what Alex wants to understand."
**Do:** click **I don't know where to start.**

### 2. Sunny explains a concept (0:20–0:45)
**Do:** type `What is an FHSA?`
**Say:** "Sunny answers first, in plain language. Under the answer, Alex can open the sources: the CRA pages this explanation is based on, with the date we reviewed them."
**Do:** open **Sources** briefly.

### 3. A spontaneous follow-up (0:45–1:00)
**Do:** type `Is an FHSA an investment?`
**Say:** "A real question people ask. Sunny separates the account's tax treatment from what's held inside it, which is the part that decides risk."

### 4. Connecting to Tangerine, honestly (1:00–1:25)
**Do:** type `Does Tangerine offer one?`
**Say:** "This is where trust is won or lost. We checked Tangerine's site and couldn't confirm an FHSA product, so Sunny says so rather than guessing, and points to what Tangerine *does* verifiably offer and how to ask them."
**Do:** click **What can Tangerine help me with?** to show the verified offerings: savings goals, TFSA options, GICs with their access conditions, and mortgage tools.

### 5. Optionally explore a scenario (1:25–1:55)
**Do:** click **Help me explore a savings goal.** → **Use my account snapshot.** → **In 1–5 years** → **Confirm** (36) → **Confirm** ($50,000) → **All of it** → **Confirm** ($700).
**Say:** "Only now does Sunny ask to use account data, because it's useful for this question. The maths is plain arithmetic in code, not generated by AI."
*(Live AI alternative: type "How much do I have saved?", tap **Yes, use my account snapshot**, then type "My target is $50,000 in 3 years. All of my savings is for the house, and I can put $700 a month toward it. How close would I get?". Verified live: $49,200, $800 short.)*

### 6. Alex corrects an assumption (1:55–2:15)
**Do:** type `Actually, keep $10,000 for emergencies`
**Say:** "Alex changes their mind. Sunny understands this as a correction, not a new topic. It keeps $10,000 separate, updates the starting amount to $14,000, and the numbers and side panel update."

### 7. A useful summary (2:15–2:35)
**Do:** click **See my summary** (Live AI: ask "Can you summarise this for me?" and click **Review your summary**).
**Say:** "Alex now knows the concepts, what's uncertain about Tangerine's offering, a realistic scenario and what to ask a person, all in a printable summary. That's a use case Tangerine could pilot in 12–18 months: a curated knowledge base, validated tools and a clear advice boundary."

---

**Backup lines if asked:** "Which investment should I buy?" shows the advice boundary. In Demo settings, **Sam** shows the no-monthly-savings branch.
**Recording tips:** keep the cursor off the chips between steps. AI replies take a few seconds, and the typing indicator covers the wait.
