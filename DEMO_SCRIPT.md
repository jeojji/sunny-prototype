# Demo script: “Alex’s money finds a purpose” (≈3½ minutes)

**Setup:** `npm run dev` → http://localhost:5173 at about 1440×900. Open **Demo settings** → **Reset all demo data** → close.
- Use **Live AI (Gemini)** if configured; Sunny’s wording will vary slightly. Rehearse once, and pace messages (free tier). If you see “high demand” or “usage limit”, wait a minute and click **Try again**.
- Use **Scripted demo** for exact wording; the chat shows a “Scripted demo” label. Say it’s scripted if you record it this way.

---

### 1. A useful moment (0:00–0:30)
**Screen:** Alex’s dashboard: balances, 90-day trend, recent activity, and Sunny’s insight card.
**Say:** “Alex didn’t open a chatbot. Sunny noticed something in Alex’s own banking: chequing has stayed above $25,000 for three months. Notice the wording: it asks, it doesn’t judge.”
**Do:** open **Why am I seeing this?** “The explanation comes from the actual data, and it’s explicit that a balance isn’t a judgment.”

### 2. Consent first (0:30–0:50)
**Do:** **Explore with Sunny** → **Yes, use my account details**.
**Say:** “Sunny asks before using account details in the conversation. *Keep it general* works too.”

### 3. What is the money actually for? (0:50–1:20)
**Do:** click **I need $3,000 for bills and $10,000 for emergencies.**
**Say:** “Sunny doesn’t assume the money is spare. It leaves $15,000 *not yet assigned to a purpose*, never ‘available to invest’, and it doesn’t quietly add the separate $12,000 in savings.” Point to the allocation card. **Yes, that’s right.**

### 4. A goal in Alex’s words (1:20–1:50)
**Do:** click **I want to build wealth, but I don’t know how investing works.**
**Say:** “No questionnaire. Sunny explains saving versus investing first, then asks one question that matters: when might Alex need this money?” Click **More than 5 years away**.

### 5. From education to Tangerine (1:50–2:25)
**Screen:** the offerings card. Expand **Tangerine Investment Funds**.
**Say:** “Here’s the bank connection: verified Tangerine offerings, each with features, fees and risks, and a link to the official page. Sunny says why they’re relevant to explore, and that this isn’t a recommendation or the only option.”
**Do:** type `What’s the difference between a GIC and an investment fund?`. “Alex can ask anything along the way.”

### 6. Alex chooses the next step (2:25–2:50)
**Do:** click **Learn about investment risk first**.
**Say:** “Alex decides what happens next, and nothing is opened or moved. Sunny remembers the choice.” Click **See my summary** and scroll: “What prompted this, what Alex confirmed, the goal, what was explored, and what’s still unknown.”

### 7. Follow-through (2:50–3:30)
**Do:** **Demo settings** → **Simulate a later visit (+30 days)**.
**Say:** “A month later, Sunny doesn’t repeat the nudge. It picks up Alex’s own next step: *‘Last time, you wanted to understand investment risk before deciding what to do.’* Alex can continue, change the goal, snooze it or stop check-ins.” Click **Continue**.
**Close:** “That’s the value of Sunny inside Tangerine: account context, a timely question, real education, product discovery and continuity toward a goal the customer set. It’s achievable in 12–18 months with a deterministic signal layer, a curated knowledge base and validated AI.”
